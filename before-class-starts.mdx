## Talking to Each Other

<Callout kind="info" collapsed="false">
  I have a neural processing disorder which makes it difficult for me to understand words if I can't see people's faces, or if people are in places with lots of background noise. You may find that I miss what you are trying to say if you only use audio or are in a noisy place - if this clashes with your own accommodations, let me know and we can work out a way to talk to each other.&#x20;
</Callout>
