## About me

Hi, I'm Clancy. I'm a professor here at UC Berkeley in the Department of Geography and the Berkeley Center for New Media. My research and teacing focuses on mapping and spatial visualization, including cartography, and I undertake a range of different projects from the theoretical to the community-engaged.

Based on decades of experience, I have a firm opinion that you cannot separate the theory (or the thinking) from the practice (or the making). This course is designed to teach you how to bring the two together in innovative and experimental ways, and to succeed you'll have to embrace the strange creative and intellectual space in-between. On the flip side, I am always open to new ideas, love a weird project and since I actually make maps, have a lot of empathy for technical catastrophe.
