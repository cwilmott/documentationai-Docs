### **Format**

Your map can be in any print-based format - folded, large, small, postcard. \
It must be in CMYK, with appropriate bleed/print marks.

You must use Illustrator for either web or desktop.

### Cartographic Exegesis

Each map must be accompanied by a short c. 1000 word essay explaining your choices and techniques used. You must include:

- At least one reference to other maps/critiques which were sources of inspiration

- An account of the cartographic elements which you did/did not use and why based on the Laying out a Page worksheet. 

- An explanation of data processing techniques (if applicable)

### Upload

Your map must be uploaded on Gradescope as *both&#x20;*

- a TIFF, and:

- an accompanying link the Creative Cloud Document either shared directly, or embedded in the essay.
