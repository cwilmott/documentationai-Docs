This assessment requires you to undertake a critical analysis of a map of a historic place or historic monument in California from the David Rumsey Archive.&#x20;

As this summative assessment is supported by the formative exercises undertaken in the labs, it is highly recommended that you use the map that you have been analysing throughout the Cartographic Theory module.

### Submission Requirements

You must hand in one PDF document designed in Adobe Illustrator for Web or Desktop, which includes:

- an annotated explanation of the elements of the map

- an outline of the history of the map, the cartographer and the purpose of the map

- a critique of the way in which the map constructs knowledge using visual techniques, inclusion or exclusion of content, and composition.

### Submission Location

Submit your map using Gradescope.
