There are three key modes of interaction which are fundamental to team work - each which require a different platform or organisational system. These are:

- **Daily and informal 1-to-1 interactions:** these are usually internal to the organisation and help make coordination easier. These interactions usually happen *in the workplace*, in meetings, via phone or short form message systems like Slack or Discord

- **Strategic and formal communications:&#x20;**&#x74;hese might be internal or external to the organisation and are official communications and agreements. These include \[all] emails, letter or fax,  signing systems like Docusign, or approval systems. All your UC Berkeley emails are eligible for Freedom of Information (FOI) requests by the general public.

- **Information management systems and documentation:&#x20;**&#x74;hese systems might be only internal or may als&#x6F;**&#xA0;**&#x62;e used to share information with external agencies, and are designed to ensur&#x65;*&#x20;continuity* beyond individuals, and make sure that if something happens other people can pick up the work. Systems might include storage systems like Google Drive, Berkeley Box, Dropbox, AWS as well as internal servers, file sharing tools like WeTransfer, documentation tools like wikis, or database systems like Airtable, Baserow or SQL. 

In order to make sure we stay on track, we will be using a specific set of tools to manage how we work in tools.

### Formal Communications

For strategic and formal communications with partners outside the University, or to document approvals and sign-offs, please use **Zoom&#x20;**&#x66;or meetings and **bMail&#x20;**&#x66;o written communication and approval sign-offs.

### Document Management

Documents will be managed via the **Google bDrive**. You will be added to the State Parks ROP Google Drive Folder - this is where we will store data, communications, assets and other materials.&#x20;

For simplicity, and to avoid having too many platforms, we will be using a **Google Sheet to manage approvals** and sign-off. This will require you to upload final approved copy, alongside the download or screenshot of the approval email.&#x20;

Your instructor will need to be cc'd into any approvals.

Finally, as documentation of your process, you will need to set up a **Notes Google Doc** in your folder where you keep track of what you are doing, notes and other information - shared with the instructor.&#x20;
