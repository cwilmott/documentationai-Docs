There is no catch-all advice for working with clients, since every project is different. But here are the questions we need to ask everytime we do, to make sure the project is successful.

### What are the stakes?

- What are the risks for the client?

- How controversial is your project?

- Why is it important, and for whom?

It's important to know what the stakes are for everyone - i.e. what happens when it goes wrong. Different organizations have different stakes and different impacts - sometimes it's a loss of trust, or the disintegration of a relationship, other times your poorly thought out map might make front page news.&#x20;

**California State Parks** is a government agency, staffed by public servants. The point of the public service (at any level) is to work for "the good of the people". People need to see themselves in their government - and if they don't, and it becomes a media storm, then the consequences for elected officials as well as public servants can be serious.&#x20;

For this reason, government organisations tend to be slow, deliberate, methodical and conservative when taking risks.&#x20;
